How to play

Controls

Arrows or A/D to move · ↑ / W / Space to jump · jump again mid-air for a double jump · ↓ / S to duck.
Land on enemies to squash them.

Ducking

Hold ↓ / S while on the ground to crouch — it shrinks your hitbox so things flying overhead (like a flyer enemy dipping down, or a shooter's projectile) pass harmlessly above you.

Goal

Reach the flag before time runs out. Collect coins for points, clear levels to unlock new ones — and new characters.

Stars

Each level can earn up to 3 stars, kept as your best-ever result:

⭐ Reach the flag ⭐ Collect every coin ⭐ Finish without losing a life

Check the 🏆 Trophies button on the main menu for bigger goals across the whole game.

Shooter enemies

The stationary green turrets fire straight ahead at head-height and flash red right before shooting. If you stay on the ground, the shot flies harmlessly over you — you don't need to dodge at all. Jumping into it is what gets you hit, so only jump once it's passed.

Characters

Each hero has a real advantage somewhere in the game — a spot only they can reach. Bolt, Tank and Pip are available immediately; Rosie, Gizmo and Nova unlock as you clear early levels.

Power-ups & pickups

🛡️ Shield — blocks one hit 💚 Life — +1 life ⚡ Speed boost 🍃 Super jump 🧲 Coin magnet 🔥 Fireball — defeat enemies on touch

Secrets

Every level hides something extra — a hard-to-reach ledge, a hidden path, or a spot only one character can use. If you bump into something odd, try again with a different hero.
